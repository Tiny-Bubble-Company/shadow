  - q: Is Shadow a Presidio alternative?
    a: "It is a complement more than a replacement. Microsoft Presidio is a detector and anonymizer library that replaces values with placeholders or hashes. Shadow adds what sits around detection: consistent, format-preserving fakes from one persona, an encrypted mapping, restoration of responses, streams and tool calls, and a ready-made HTTPS proxy and gateway. Shadow can call Presidio as an extra detector. See [Shadow vs Presidio](/shadow/compare/presidio/)."
  - q: Does Shadow work with Claude Code, Cursor or GitHub Copilot?
    a: "Claude Code and other tools that accept a base URL (`ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`, `OPENAI_BASE_URL`) or honour `HTTPS_PROXY` work through [gateway mode](/shadow/integrate/gateway-mode/) or [proxy mode](/shadow/integrate/proxy-mode/), and the `code` intent keeps identifiers readable. Cursor and GitHub Copilot send traffic to their vendors' own backends, which are not on Shadow's built-in host list; you can add hosts with `extra_hosts`, but check the request log before relying on it, and clients that pin certificates cannot be intercepted.   - q: Is Shadow GDPR or HIPAA compliant?

Whether you are compliant still depends on your whole programme: contracts, policies, access control and what else you send. See [industries](/shadow/industries/)."
  - q: Does Shadow work offline and self-hosted?
    a: "Yes. Shadow is a single binary that runs on your laptops, servers or containers, with no cloud service, account or telemetry. Detection, the persona and the encrypted vault are all local, and optional detectors (a NER model, Presidio) run on addresses you choose. The only outbound traffic is the masked request you were already sending, and you can point it at a self-hosted model endpoint instead of a cloud provider."
  - q: Can Shadow unmask responses and tool calls?
    a: "Yes. Every fake in the response is turned back into the real value, including streamed SSE and NDJSON responses where a fake is split across events. Tool-call arguments are restored on your machine before your tools run, so an agent's email reaches the real address. Tools hosted in the provider's cloud are handled by an explicit policy. See [tool calls and agents](/shadow/concepts/tool-calls/)."
  - q: What does Shadow cost?
    a: "Nothing. Shadow is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with no account, licence key or usage fees. You keep paying your AI provider as before."
  - q: How accurate is Shadow's PII detection?
    a: "Detection combines checksummed patterns (cards, IBANs, national IDs), secrets and machine identifiers, your own dictionary of names and codenames, and an optional local NER model or Presidio for free-text names. Results depend on your data, so Shadow publishes its methodology and measurements on the [benchmarks](/shadow/benchmarks/) page, and `shadow scan` shows what would be masked in your own files before you rely on it."
  - q: Will masking make the AI's answers worse?
    a: "Usually not, because the model still sees coherent data: a fake email is still an email, two different people stay two different people, and a fake card still passes Luhn. That is the main difference from placeholder redaction, which breaks the model's ability to reason about the values. For bulk reports Shadow switches to typed tokens such as `[PERSON_0001]`, which are restored in the answer."
  - q: Which AI providers does Shadow support?
    a: "OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and Vertex AI, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, Together AI, DeepSeek, xAI, Cohere, Perplexity and more, plus any custom or self-hosted HTTPS model API you add. See [supported providers](/shadow/reference/providers/)."

## What is Shadow?

**Shadow is an open-source privacy layer that sits between your apps and AI providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Gemini.** It finds personal data, customer names, credentials and internal identifiers in every outbound prompt, replaces them with realistic, format-preserving fakes, and translates the fakes back in the response. It is PII redaction and AI data loss prevention that keeps ChatGPT-style assistants, Claude, coding agents and your own LLM apps useful.

Shadow runs where your data is: as a local HTTPS proxy for every tool on a machine, as a gateway base URL for SDKs and services, or inside your Rust service as the `shadow-core` library. The mapping between real and fake values lives in an encrypted vault on that machine and never leaves it.

## How does Shadow stop data leaks to AI?

<Steps>

1. **Intercept.** Your tool sends its request through Shadow, via `HTTPS_PROXY`, a base URL such as `/gw/openai/v1`, or the Rust API. Only known AI hosts are decrypted; everything else is tunnelled untouched.

2. **Detect.** Checksummed patterns (cards, IBANs, national IDs), emails, phones, secrets and API keys, hostnames, IPs and paths, your own dictionary of customers and codenames, and optionally a local NER model or Presidio find the sensitive spans, including inside uploaded DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and PDF files.

3. **Translate.** An intent-aware policy picks a treatment per value: a consistent fake from your machine's persona, a typed token for bulk reports, the real value for an action target, or, if you opt in, irreversible redaction for secrets.

4. **Restore.** The provider answers about a company that does not exist. Shadow turns every fake back into the real value, in full responses, streamed tokens and tool-call arguments, before your tool sees it.

</Steps>

Read the full walkthrough in [How it works](/shadow/concepts/how-it-works/).

## How is Shadow different from DLP that blocks?

Most data loss prevention for AI either **blocks** the request or **redacts** values with placeholders such as `<PERSON>`. Blocking breaks the tool, so people route around it. Placeholders keep the request flowing but destroy meaning: the model cannot tell two people apart, write to the right address or keep a table consistent. Shadow **translates** instead, so the request goes through, the model reasons about coherent data, and you get a useful answer about your real data.
