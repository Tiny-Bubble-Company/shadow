faq:
  - q: How do I get help with Shadow?
    a: "Email support@getshadow.dev with questions about installing, configuring or rolling out Shadow. Bugs and feature requests are welcome as GitHub issues, and security vulnerabilities should be reported privately as described in the security policy."
  - q: How do I stop employees pasting customer data into ChatGPT?
    a: "Give people an AI tool that is safe to paste into instead of one they have to avoid. Route your API-based AI clients (internal chat apps, SDK-based tools, coding assistants, agents) through Shadow, and customer names, emails, IDs and secrets are replaced with fakes before the request leaves the machine, while the answer still comes back with the real values. Shadow intercepts provider APIs such as `api.openai.com` and `api.anthropic.com`, and with the [system proxy](/shadow/integrate/proxy-mode/#system-wide-with-the-system-proxy) turned on it also masks ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek and Grok in a browser. Phone apps and chat apps it does not support yet, such as Microsoft Copilot, are not covered, so pair it with a policy for those. See [use cases](/shadow/use-cases/)."
  - q: Is Shadow a Presidio alternative?
    a: "It is a complement more than a replacement. Microsoft Presidio is a detector and anonymizer library that replaces values with placeholders or hashes. Shadow adds what sits around detection: consistent, format-preserving fakes from one persona, an encrypted mapping, restoration of responses, streams and tool calls, and a ready-made HTTPS proxy and gateway. Shadow can call Presidio as an extra detector. See [Shadow vs Presidio](/shadow/compare/presidio/)."
  - q: Does Shadow work with Claude Code, Cursor or GitHub Copilot?
    a: "Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot and other tools that accept a base URL (`ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL`, `OPENAI_BASE_URL`) or honour `HTTPS_PROXY` work through [gateway mode](/shadow/integrate/gateway-mode/) or [proxy mode](/shadow/integrate/proxy-mode/), and the `code` intent keeps identifiers readable. Cursor and Windsurf use their own binary protocols through their vendors' servers, which Shadow cannot rewrite: it recognises that traffic and shows it as *seen, not protected* instead. Clients that pin certificates cannot be intercepted. See the [catalog of AI services](/shadow/reference/providers/#the-catalog)."
  - q: Is Shadow GDPR or HIPAA compliant?
    a: "No tool makes an organisation compliant on its own. Shadow helps you meet data-minimisation, confidentiality and transfer requirements under GDPR and HIPAA by replacing the personal data and PHI it detects before requests reach AI providers, and its mapping never leaves your machine. Detection is best effort, and an action the provider runs itself (a hosted tool) is sent the real contact details it needs, each one logged. Whether you are compliant still depends on your whole programme: contracts, policies, access control and what else you send. See [industries](/shadow/industries/)."
  - q: Does Shadow work offline and self-hosted?
    a: "Yes. Shadow is a single binary that runs on your laptops, servers or containers, with no cloud service, account or telemetry. Detection, the persona and the encrypted vault are all local, and optional detectors (a NER model, Presidio) run on addresses you choose. The only outbound traffic is the masked request you were already sending, and you can point it at a self-hosted model endpoint instead of a cloud provider."
  - q: Can Shadow unmask responses and tool calls?
    a: "Yes. Every fake in the response is turned back into the real value, including streamed SSE and NDJSON responses where a fake is split across events. Tool-call arguments are restored on your machine before your tools run. When you ask the model to write to or act on someone, that recipient is passed through as written (and logged), so an agent's email reaches the real address and greets the right person. Tools hosted in the provider's cloud are handled by an explicit policy. See [tool calls and agents](/shadow/concepts/tool-calls/)."
  - q: What does Shadow cost?
    a: "Nothing. Shadow is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence, with no account, licence key or usage fees. You keep paying your AI provider as before."
  - q: How accurate is Shadow's PII detection?
    a: "Detection combines checksummed patterns (cards, IBANs, national IDs), secrets and machine identifiers, your own dictionary of names and codenames, and an optional local NER model or Presidio for free-text names. Results depend on your data, so Shadow publishes its methodology and measurements on the [benchmarks](/shadow/benchmarks/) page, and `shadow scan` shows what would be masked in your own files before you rely on it."
  - q: Will masking make the AI's answers worse?
    a: "Usually not, because the model still sees coherent data: a fake email is still an email, two different people stay two different people, and a fake card still passes Luhn. That is the main difference from placeholder redaction, which breaks the model's ability to reason about the values. For bulk reports Shadow switches to typed tokens such as `[PERSON_0001]`, which are restored in the answer."
  - q: Which AI providers does Shadow support?
    a: "OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and Vertex AI, Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, Together AI, DeepSeek, xAI, Cohere, Perplexity and more, plus any custom or self-hosted HTTPS model API you add. See [supported providers](/shadow/reference/providers/)."
