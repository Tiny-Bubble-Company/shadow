You're here because you ran `shadow hub connect` or opened an invite from your organisation. This page explains what connecting does before anything is sent.

## Connect

With the invite link from your organisation:

```sh
shadow hub connect 'https://hub.example.com/join/inv_...'
```

Shadow shows the organisation's name and what it will see, and asks you to confirm. Nothing is sent before you say yes. On a new machine, the one-line installer from the invite page installs Shadow and connects it in one go.

## What is sent

Your organisation chooses one sharing level, and Shadow applies it **on your machine** before anything leaves it:

| Level | Sent to your organisation |
|---|---|
| Counts | Which AI services and apps were used, how many values of each type Shadow protected, what was blocked. No text. |
| Sanitised | The above, plus each incident's prompt **as the AI received it** (real names, emails and other values already replaced with fakes), the model and the app's process. |
| Full | The above, plus the original prompt and values, and conversation ids the app sent. |

Every level includes your machine's name, operating system, user name and the person it is enrolled to.

## What is never sent

- Traffic that is not going to an AI service: none of your other browsing or app traffic is recorded, at any level.
- Anything from before you connected.
- Your Shadow vault, persona or keys.

`shadow status`, the menu-bar app and the local dashboard always show which organisation manages the machine and the current level.

## Disconnect

```sh
shadow hub disconnect
```

Shadow stops reporting immediately and keeps protecting the machine. Ask an admin to remove the machine from Hub as well.
